**Description:**
ASTRA VITAL is an offline-first health monitoring prototype that helps astronauts track health indicators, understand trends and spot concerning changes during long-duration missions — even without a link to Earth. A transparent, rule-based engine explains every alert (what changed, why it matters, what to do, when to contact the flight surgeon) and links each indicator to NASA's five hazards of human spaceflight. The prototype also explores real laboratory data from NASA's Open Science Data Repository (Inspiration4: OSD-575, OSD-569).
